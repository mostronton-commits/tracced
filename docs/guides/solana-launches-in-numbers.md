# Solana launches in numbers

What 159 analysed tokens say about who buys before a pump, who sells into it, and who takes the profit. Counted on
November 28, 2026 from every public analysis on tracced: one per token, 52,124 wallets, ranges from mid-September to late
November 2026.

!!! note "📐 What this sample is"
    People chose these tokens and marked these ranges, usually the minutes right before a pump. So the sample leans toward
    tokens that did pump. A wallet's numbers come from all its swaps on the token, not only the ones inside the range.
    [How a result is counted](/docs/how-it-works).

## The short version

| | |
|---|---|
| Wallets that bought in a marked range, median per token | **92** |
| Length of a marked range, median | **3 minutes** |
| Wallets with a realized profit | **57%** |
| Wallets that sold at 2× their entry or better | **13.5%** |
| Wallets that sold at 10× or better | **1.1%** |
| Wallets that sold everything | **66%** |
| How long those held, median | **7 minutes** |
| Share of a token's profit taken by its top 10% of wallets, median | **67%** |
| Share taken by the single best wallet, median | **15%** |

## Most early buyers make a little, a few make most of it

More than half of the wallets that bought before a pump closed with some profit, but the median realized profit was
**$5.79** on a median position of **$175**. The money sits at the top: in a typical token the best tenth of the wallets took two
thirds of all the profit, and one wallet alone took about a sixth.

Selling fast is the norm. Two thirds of early buyers had sold out by the time of the analysis, and half of those held less
than seven minutes. A quarter held under a minute and a half.

## Snipers, fresh wallets and bundles are everywhere

| Found in a range | Tokens | Share of all wallets |
|---|---|---|
| a `sniper`, bought within 60 s of the token's creation | 85% | 15% |
| a `fresh` wallet, under a day old at its first buy | 93% | 3.5% |
| a `bundle`: three or more wallets first funded by one address | 43% | 2.4% |
| a bot-like wallet | 67% | 2.8% |
| a wallet inside the creator's own launch bundle | 3.8% | under 0.1% |

Almost every token had at least one fresh wallet among its early buyers, and four in ten had a funding cluster. These are
small groups: a few wallets among dozens. [What each tag means](/docs/tags).

## Those groups do better than everyone else

| Group | In profit | Median realized |
|---|---|---|
| wallets in a funding cluster (`bundle`) | 77% | $236 |
| fresh wallets | 74% | $141 |
| snipers | 63% | $13 |
| everyone else | 56–57% | about $5 |

A wallet that shares its funder with two others, or that was created the day it bought, made 30 to 45 times the median
profit of an ordinary early buyer. That is the case for checking who is behind a launch before you buy it: see
[Is it bundled?](/guides/check-if-a-coin-is-bundled).

They rarely own the whole top, though. Among the 20 most profitable wallets of a token, the median number that were fresh
or bundled was one. Only 3 of 144 tokens had ten or more.

## The creator in the range

In 65 of the 159 tokens (41%) the wallet that created the token's pool was itself among the buyers of the marked range. 19
of those sold on the token and 14 sold everything. See [Dev sold](/guides/dev-sold).

## Check a token yourself

Paste its address on [tracced](/), mark the minutes before the pump on the chart, and press **Get wallets**. Every row is a
wallet, with what it paid, sold and earned, and every trade opens on Solscan. Finished analyses are free to open for
anyone.
