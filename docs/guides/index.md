# Guides

Short answers to what traders ask about Solana memecoin launches. Each one shows how to check it by hand and on tracced,
with numbers from 159 tokens analysed on tracced from September to November 2026.

| Guide | The question |
|---|---|
| [Is it bundled?](/guides/check-if-a-coin-is-bundled) | How to check whether one hand bought the launch from many wallets |
| [Insider wallets](/guides/find-insider-wallets) | How to find who bought before the pump, step by step |
| [Dev sold](/guides/dev-sold) | What the badge means and what it does not see |
| [Bundles vs snipers](/guides/jito-bundles-snipers) | Jito bundles, bundlers, funding clusters and snipers, told apart |
| [Copy trading](/guides/copy-trading-early-buyers) | Whether copying the first buyers works, and whom to follow |
| [Launches in numbers](/guides/solana-launches-in-numbers) | Who buys before a pump, who sells into it, who takes the profit |

Every number here comes from wallets' own swaps on chain. tracced shows no scores and no "smart money" labels: each row of a
result opens on Solscan. [How a result is counted](/docs/how-it-works).
