How it works

tracced shows who bought a token before its pump and what they did next — as facts from the token's own trades. No scores, no "smart money" labels. You decide who is worth following.

Where the data comes from

  • Raw swaps of the token from Solana Tracker — the same transactions you can open on Solscan. Every number is computed from them; nothing comes from third-party PnL or scores. Completeness is verified against the chain.
  • Market cap of a trade = price × token supply.
  • Wallet age and funder — from the public Solana RPC: the wallet's oldest transaction.
  • Candles — Solana Tracker, shown in market cap.

Range and scope

The range (From → To) is yours to set on the chart: usually from where buying starts to where the pump takes off. It only selects wallets — every wallet that bought inside it is in the table.

The numbers come from all of a wallet's trades on the token, before, inside and after the range. The scope switch recounts the same trades up to the analysis time, or 24 h / 48 h after the range. Entry cap is always the wallet's first buy inside the range, so it does not move with the scope.

Columns

Entry cap
Market cap at the first buy inside the range · its time
In range
USD spent inside the range · total on the token, if different
Exit cap
Average market cap of the sells · first sell time
Got
USD received from sells · share of bought tokens sold
Realized
Profit on the sold part (average cost) · unrealized value of the rest at the last price
×
Average exit cap ÷ average entry cap
Held
Minutes from the entry to the first sell after it · buys / sells

Complete data, cheapest path

The range is always fetched in full. For each wallet's history the app takes the cheaper of two complete paths: the whole trade history of the token, or each wallet's own trades. Only when neither fits the budget ({{ s.max_wallet_lookups }} wallets on the {{ s.plan }} plan) do some wallets stay without exits — they get the no-exits tag, and the line above the table says so.

Limits

  • Range up to {{ s.max_window_hours }} h.
  • One run may use at most {{ s.budget_guard_pct }}% of the requests left this month; the app stops before spending more.
  • Wallets that spent less than ${{ s.min_invested_usd }} in the range are left out.
  • Wallet age is checked for up to {{ s.age_lookups_max }} wallets per analysis, in the background.
  • Chart markers: up to 3 wallets at once, {{ s.markers_max }} trades each.

The AI agent Coming next

It will read the facts of the visible rows and your method, and return a short list of wallets with a reason for each. It sees nothing but that table and does not predict. Verify before acting.

Export

CSV, TXT and JSON export the same wallets: the selected ones, or the filtered rows if nothing is selected. CSV has all columns and tags; TXT has addresses only; JSON adds the range and coverage.

Your account

Sign in with a Solana wallet. The wallet signs a short message with a one-time code — no transaction, no fees — and that signature is your account: no password, no e-mail. Tick wallets in a result and Save to my list; Save analysis keeps the whole result. Both live under My list, with notes and export.
